About Me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in ISEP and the Department of Mathematics at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Vincent Y.F. Tan. Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Statistics and B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where my master's advisor was Prof. Hong Zhang.

I am interested in improving the performance and efficiency of large language models by understanding how model architectures and learning algorithms interact with training data. My current focus is on full versus hybrid attention in pretraining and long-context mid-training, and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) versus reinforcement learning (RL) for reasoning. My research also spans in-context learning and reinforcement learning theory.

From May to August 2026, I interned with the Pretrain Group at Wizard Intelligence Learning Lab, working on efficient long-context LLMs with Dr. Dachao Lin.

See my research page for publications, preprints, and industry experience.
