Research Interests

I study how model architectures and learning algorithms interact with training data to improve the performance and efficiency of large language models. My current interests include full versus hybrid attention in pretraining and long-context mid-training, SFT versus RL for reasoning, in-context learning, and reinforcement learning theory.
